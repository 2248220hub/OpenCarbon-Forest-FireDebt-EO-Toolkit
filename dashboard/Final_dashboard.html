Arischia forest fire · L'Aquila, Abruzzo · 2020-07-30 to 2020-08-14

V. Emberumal · MSc Space & Astronautical Eng., Sapienza · Earth Observation

Six novelty findings

1. Zero CH₄ retrievals0 of 27 days valid; CO returned 33. Predicted 0.079 σ before looking.
2. Detection floor27–1,098 t CH₄/day across 27 geometries. Max achievable σ = 0.47.
3. Diurnal duty cycle14.5 % — about 3.5 equivalent full-power hours per day.
4. Methane debt47–140 years of the forest's own uptake, released in 15 days.
5. Carbon closure104.6 % — emission factors and carbon fraction are consistent.
6. Not a drought fireNDMI -0.66 σ · 180.6 mm rain · 18.1 °C before ignition. Ignition-limited, not fuel-limited.
